TeamFlow

A project case study

Teamflow is a conceptual SaaS product built around helping teams preserve the context behind their work. I developed its marketing website, shaping the product's narrative, visual identity, content structure and frontend implementation to communicate its value proposition effectively.

View live site Read the story
01
THE CHALLENGE

An unfamiliar product needs to be understood first.

TeamFlow isn't another chat tool or project management app. It's a decision-context layer, which is a new and less familiar category. The challenge was to design a marketing website that could quickly make the concept clear, relevant and valuable to the right audience.

02
THE APPROACH

Build understanding before showing features.

The homepage is structured to progressively build understanding: problem → impact → shift → product.

01. Problem

Framing the core friction teams experience before introducing any solution or tool features.

02. Impact

Quantifying the hidden cost and inefficiency of disjointed organizational context.

03. Shift

Introducing the paradigm mindset shift required to align team context seamlessly.

04. Product

Revealing the actual TeamFlow interface operating as the live context hub.

03
THE RESULT

A marketing website that brings the product to life.

The final result is a clean, focused marketing website that explains an unfamiliar product, uses the interface to show how it works, and reflects a modern, considered visual style.
